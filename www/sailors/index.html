Get it installed

Set it up before you leave the dock.

AudioChart installs like any app — no App Store required. Do this while you've still got Wi‑Fi.

iPhone / iPad
  1. Open egilchri.github.io/AudioChart in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.

Must use Safari — not Chrome — for this to work on iOS.

Android
  1. Open egilchri.github.io/AudioChart in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then confirm.
Desktop
  1. Open egilchri.github.io/AudioChart in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar (or the browser menu).
  3. Confirm — it opens in its own window from then on.
