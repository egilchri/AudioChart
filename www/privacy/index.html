Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

In short: AudioChart has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your routes, tracks, waypoints and settings stay in your own browser. A few map, tide and lookup services see your connection and the area you're viewing, and Google Drive is used only if you turn on Sync.

What stays on your device

Everything you create or set in AudioChart is stored in your browser's own storage on that device: routes, tracks, waypoints, Test Sets, boat draft and other settings, and downloaded chart data for offline use. It is not sent to us. There is no AudioChart account and no AudioChart server that collects your data.

Your GPS position is used on your device to show where you are, plan routes and give spoken bearings. AudioChart does not send your position to anyone. (If you run AudioChart's optional companion server on your own computer, the app can send its position to that computer, which you control.)

Services the app talks to

To show maps and live information, your browser contacts these services directly. Like any website, they receive your IP address and the details of what was requested:

  • Map pictures: OpenStreetMap (street map) and Esri (satellite) receive requests for the map tiles covering the area you're looking at, or the area you download for offline use.
  • Tides and currents: NOAA's Tides & Currents service receives requests for the stations near your area.
  • Geology and town lookups: the Maine GeoLibrary (hosted on ArcGIS) receives the map area or the point you tap when you use the Geology or Towns map types.
  • Google Maps: only if you tap a "view in Google Maps" link, which opens that location in Google Maps.
  • Hosting: the app is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy policy.

AudioChart doesn't control these services. Their own privacy policies apply.

Spoken announcements (bearings, hazards) use your browser's built-in text-to-speech voices. AudioChart prefers voices that run on your device, but some browser voices (for example Chrome's online "Google" voices) are generated by the browser maker's servers, which then receive the text being spoken. AudioChart does not use your microphone.

Google Drive Sync (optional)

Sync is off until you tap ☁ Sync and sign in with Google. If you do:

  • AudioChart asks for two Drive permissions. App data lets it keep one backup file of your routes, tracks and Test Sets in a hidden folder in your own Drive that only AudioChart can see. Files you choose lets Import from Drive open files you pick yourself. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.
  • Waypoints are not included in the backup.
  • The backup goes straight from your browser to your Google Drive. We never receive it.
  • The sign-in only lasts while AudioChart is open. It isn't stored, and it ends when you close or reload the app.
  • To remove access and the backup, go to your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps, or Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, and remove AudioChart.

AudioChart's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Cookies and tracking

AudioChart sets no tracking cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or fingerprinting. Browser storage is used only to keep your own data and settings, and to make the app work offline.

Deleting your data

Clear AudioChart's site data in your browser's settings, or delete the installed app. That removes everything stored on that device. If you used Sync, also remove AudioChart's access in your Google account as described above, which deletes its hidden backup file.

Children

AudioChart is a boating tool, not directed at children, and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. AudioChart's source code is public, so you can check what it does yourself.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/egilchri/AudioChart/issues.

Safety

Navigate with care. AudioChart is an aid to navigation. Use it alongside official charts, your own navigation equipment and good seamanship, not instead of them. See the notice shown when you first open the app.
