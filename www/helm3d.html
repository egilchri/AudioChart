    Drag the view to look around, scroll to zoom like binoculars, Space to play or pause. While a Virtual Journey of this route runs in AudioChart, this view follows it. At the end of the route the anchor goes down (click once in this window first so the browser allows the sound). Eye height 2.0 m at the tiller. Land is USGS elevation (10 m near the route, about 37 m beyond) with tree height added where the photo shows forest, since the elevation data is bare earth. Photos are a single survey summer, so moored boats and shadows are frozen in place. Buoys are drawn up to 6× real size at a distance so they stay visible. Desktop browser with WebGL needed; the first run of a route downloads 20–40 MB.
