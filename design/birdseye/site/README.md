# Ted's Birdseye, from AudioChart

https://egilchri.github.io/birdseye/

Bird's-eye on its own: the bays from the air in 3D, installable as an app of its own. The view is AudioChart's
Bird's-eye (https://egilchri.github.io/AudioChart/bay3d.html?app=birdseye), shown full-window; this site is just
the page around it, its manifest, icons and service worker.

Source: `design/birdseye/site/` in https://github.com/egilchri/AudioChart (edit there, then copy here).
