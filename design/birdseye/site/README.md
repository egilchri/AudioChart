# Ted's Birdseye, from AudioChart

https://egilchri.github.io/birdseye/

Bird's-eye on its own: the bays from the air in 3D, installable as an app of its own. The view is AudioChart's
Bird's-eye (https://egilchri.github.io/AudioChart/bay3d.html?app=birdseye), shown full-window; this site is just
the page around it, its manifest, icons and service worker.

Source: `design/birdseye/site/` in https://github.com/egilchri/AudioChart (edit there, then copy here).

`tour.html` (2026-11-02): "San Francisco Bay, live", the narrated tour video (`tour/sf-live.mp4`), recorded from
Bird's-eye with the live feeds by AudioChart's `design/pending/live-tour/` (`record.mjs`, then `build.py`).

`tour/nyc-buses.mp4` (2026-11-22): "New York's buses, live", added to the tour page above San Francisco; made by
AudioChart's `design/pending/bus-video/` (`record.mjs`, then `build.py`).

`tour/nyc-met.mp4` (2026-11-22): "Brooklyn to the Met, by subway and bus", at the top of the tour page; made by
AudioChart's `design/pending/met-tour/` (`record.mjs`, then `build.py`).
