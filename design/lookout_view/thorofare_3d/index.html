Drag the view to look around, scroll to zoom like binoculars, Space to play or pause. Eye height 2.5 m. Land is USGS elevation (10 m around Rockland and the Thorofare, 30 m beyond) with tree height added where the aerial photo shows forest, since the elevation data is bare earth. Photos are from USDA's NAIP survey, so moored boats and shadows are frozen as they were that summer. Buoys are drawn up to 6× real size at a distance so they stay visible. Rockland Breakwater isn't in the elevation data, so it's traced from the aerial photo. Desktop browser with WebGL needed.
